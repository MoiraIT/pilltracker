Welcome to PillTracker

PillTracker reminds you when to take your medicines and keeps a simple record of what you took.

  • Everything stays on this device. Nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Reminders sound while PillTracker is open. To get reminders when it is closed, add them to your phone's calendar from the caregiver settings.
  • PillTracker does not replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist.
